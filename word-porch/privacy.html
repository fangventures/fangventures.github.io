WORD PORCH · FANG VENTURES

Privacy policy

Effective November 8, 2026 · Updated November 23, 2026

Word Porch is created by Victor Fang and published under the Fang Ventures name (the Word Porch publisher, “we” or “us”). This policy covers these Word Porch web pages, the sample puzzle, and the iPhone, iPad, Android, and browser games.

At a glance

Your saved game stays on your device. In the mobile apps, you can choose to share gameplay and purchase activity with us through Google Analytics for Firebase. Apple or Google Play handles purchases on its platform, and Google AdMob serves ads.

Privacy questions or requests: FangVentures.HQ@gmail.com.

1. Progress and preferences

The game saves puzzle progress, postcards, points, scores, streaks, and settings locally. Browser play uses website storage and an offline cache; the native app uses local app storage. Word Porch has no player account, cloud save, or online leaderboard. If you choose to share play metrics, selected events from your game are sent as described below.

On iPhone and iPad, the points wallet and purchase/reward delivery identifiers are stored in the device Keychain to prevent duplicate credits and recover interrupted delivery. This wallet is device-only and can remain after app removal. Progress backups exclude points, scores, and purchase ownership.

On Android, the points wallet and purchase/reward delivery records are stored privately on the device and excluded from app backups. Clearing Word Porch’s storage or uninstalling the app removes this wallet, including unused purchased points. There is no Word Porch cloud wallet or automatic balance transfer between devices or between Apple and Android. Eligible store purchases can be checked separately through Restore purchases; this does not recover a lost points balance.

The current game does not request precise location, contacts, camera, or microphone access. On iPhone and iPad, photo permission is requested only when you choose Save to Photos. Word Porch uses add-only access to save the postcard and cannot read your existing photos. On Android 10 and later, saving a postcard adds the generated image to your pictures without photo-library read permission. Older Android versions may request storage permission to save it; Word Porch does not read existing photos. Import and share actions use files or system options you choose.

Optional play metrics in the mobile apps

You can play with either choice. If you choose Share metrics, we use Google Analytics for Firebase to see where players get stuck, whether they return and what leads them to a purchase. We collect the game and app version, time spent playing, puzzle progress, guess outcomes and hint use. We also collect how you reached the shop, offers you viewed, checkout outcomes and verified purchase observations or, where supported by the platform, revocations. Purchase events can include the product, a coded transaction reference and, when available, the price and currency.

Google Analytics uses an app-instance identifier to recognize return visits and processes app/device information and approximate location. Our custom play metrics leave out your profile name, the words you enter and your postcard greetings. On Android, we disable advertising-ID collection and ad-personalization features for Firebase Analytics. This setting is separate from AdMob’s advertising data described below. Read more about Google Analytics data collection.

2. Advertising in the native app

The iPhone, iPad, and Android games integrate Google AdMob banners, full-screen ads at game breaks, and optional rewarded videos. The separate Remove Ads purchase hides banners and automatic ads. Players who bought an older destination pack from the App Store with that benefit keep it. Optional rewarded videos remain available. Test builds may show Google test ads. The Word Porch marketing pages and sample puzzle do not load AdMob; the browser game uses labeled ad previews.

The mobile apps request non-personalized ads and disable publisher first-party IDs. The iPhone and iPad app does not request App Tracking Transparency permission or access to IDFA. On Android, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may use an available Android advertising ID and other device or app identifiers. Android’s system settings provide advertising-ID controls. Non-personalized ads still involve data processing. Google’s SDK may process IP addresses and approximate location, app- or device-bounded identifiers, ad views and interactions, crash reports, and performance information for advertising, measurement, security, and fraud prevention. We do not send your puzzle progress, postcards, or greetings to the ad SDK.

Production ad requests are gated by Google’s User Messaging Platform consent status. Required messages and available privacy choices depend on your region and Google’s configuration. In the game, open the Word Porch menu → Help → Privacy choices when shown. Ad availability also depends on connectivity and inventory.

See Google’s privacy policy and Google’s iOS SDK data disclosures and Android SDK data disclosures.

3. Store purchases

Apple StoreKit handles iPhone and iPad payments. Word Porch receives verified product, transaction, and ownership information to unlock destination packs, deliver points, remove ads, and process refunds or revocations. We do not receive your payment-card details. Apple may process purchase and account data under its privacy policy.

Google Play Billing handles available Android purchases. Word Porch receives product, signed purchase, token, and ownership information to verify and deliver purchases and track completed delivery. Signed ownership records are stored locally; custom play metrics use a coded purchase reference rather than the original purchase token. We do not receive payment-card details. Google processes store and account information under its privacy policy. This website does not accept payments.

4. Sharing and exported files

Sharing is optional. The game creates postcards and score cards locally. A shared card or link may include artwork, a puzzle code, destination, score or streak, and an optional greeting you enter. A progress backup contains the progress and settings you choose to export.

When you save or send a file, the chosen device, storage provider, or messaging app receives it under its own privacy practices. We do not automatically post, message friends, or upload contacts. Share only information you intend recipients to see.

5. Website visits and support email

These Word Porch pages have no added analytics scripts, advertising pixels, or third-party fonts. The sample keeps answers in memory until the page reloads; only your optional larger-text preference is saved in localStorage.

GitHub Pages and its delivery infrastructure receive ordinary request data, such as IP addresses, requested pages, and browser headers, to serve and secure the site. See GitHub’s privacy statement. External links have their own privacy practices.

If you email us, we and our email provider receive your address, message, and any attachments. We use them to answer requests, troubleshoot, and maintain necessary support records. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or unrelated sensitive information.

6. Retention, deletion, and controls

While play metrics are on, the app also keeps a local event log that works offline. Event entries older than 14 days are cleared when the app next maintains the log. In the Android release, events are sent through Google Analytics for Firebase; the separate upload of the local raw-event log is disabled. The local log’s retention period is not a guarantee that every offline event will appear in Analytics reports.

To withdraw consent, open the Word Porch menu → Settings → Play metrics and choose Keep private. This stops new play metrics and clears the local metrics log and its identifier. The change applies going forward and does not automatically remove information already received by Google. Contact us about previously sent metrics. This choice controls optional play metrics; advertising and store purchases have separate data practices.

Local game records remain until removed through browser or device controls. Clearing site data can erase browser progress and cached files. On Android, clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the app’s local wallet, progress, and metrics records. On iPhone and iPad, removing the app can erase progress while Keychain wallet records may persist. We have no Word Porch cloud account from which to recover or remotely erase your device’s records. Contact us for help with a deletion request; we cannot promise that uninstalling the iPhone or iPad app clears its Keychain records. Files you exported or saved to Photos can remain separately.

We retain support correspondence only as reasonably needed to handle the request, maintain necessary business records, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. Hosting, store, email, and advertising providers apply their own retention policies. We use reasonable safeguards; no storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Use available ad privacy controls to change consent where offered. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to access, correct, delete, obtain a copy of, restrict, or object to processing; withdraw consent; opt out of legally defined sale or sharing; or complain to a privacy regulator. Email us to exercise applicable rights or appeal a request decision. We may need reasonable information to verify a request. We do not penalize you for exercising applicable rights. Requests about Apple’s or Google’s own records may need to go to that provider.

We do not sell puzzle progress or support messages. Advertising-related processing can fall within legal definitions of sharing in some regions; use available privacy choices or contact us for assistance.

7. Processing purposes and location

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, we rely as applicable on providing requested services, legitimate interests in support and security, legal obligations, or consent for processing that requires it. Withdrawing consent does not affect earlier lawful processing. Our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries under their privacy terms and applicable transfer safeguards. Contact us with questions about a specific provider or processing activity.

8. Children, changes, and contact

Word Porch is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can address it.

We will update the date on this page when practices change and provide any additional notice or consent required by law. For Word Porch privacy matters, contact Fang Ventures at FangVentures.HQ@gmail.com.
