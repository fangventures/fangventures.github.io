Privacy Policy

Who provides the game?

Rocket Loader is published by Fang Ventures, a brand of Fang Ventures Global LLC, Austin, Texas, USA. For privacy questions or requests, email FangVentures.HQ@gmail.com.

Local game data and optional profiles

The game stores level progress, moves, stars, personal bests, settings, coin balances, destination unlocks, reward claims and purchase-delivery identifiers on your device or in browser storage. These records support play, resume your progress and prevent duplicate rewards or purchase delivery. They are not uploaded to a publisher-operated game server. There is no game account or cloud-wallet service.

You can play as a guest. An optional profile stores your chosen name and built-in character or selected photo locally. A selected photo is cropped and re-encoded on your device; the game does not upload it automatically. Gameplay does not require contacts, precise location, microphone or camera access.

The current game has no publisher-operated gameplay-analytics service. Advertising providers can still process usage and diagnostic information as described below.

Advertising and consent

The iOS app integrates Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). UMP checks consent status and shows required privacy messages before eligible ad requests. Ads are requested as non-personalized inventory. This does not mean that no data is processed. The browser preview does not load native AdMob ads.

Google’s advertising and consent services may process IP addresses and approximate location, app or device identifiers, consent choices, ad views and interactions, app interactions, crash information and performance diagnostics. Uses include delivering and measuring ads, handling consent, operating the services and preventing fraud. The precise processing depends on the SDK version, configuration, region and your choices.

The game does not request Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission or intentionally access the advertising identifier. Other SDK processing can still occur. See Google’s privacy policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures.

We require providers processing information for us to protect it consistently with this policy and applicable privacy requirements. Providers may also process information under their own policies for their services.

Apple purchases

Apple handles checkout and payment details. The game receives product, price, verified transaction and entitlement information to deliver purchases, prevent duplicate coin credit and recognize ad-removal ownership. The local wallet retains processed transaction identifiers. Fang Ventures does not receive your payment-card details through the game.

Apple maintains its own transaction records under Apple’s privacy policy. Restoring eligible Remove Ads ownership does not recover delivered consumable coins or local game saves. Our purchase support guide explains recovery limits.

Sharing is your choice

Score pictures are generated locally. If your profile is enabled, a picture or share text may include your name, avatar and badge, plus the mission result. The public game link does not encode your private name or photo. Native sharing uses a temporary image file that is removed after the share sheet finishes.

Nothing is posted automatically. When you choose an external app or social service, it receives the content you send and applies its own policies. Use Guest mode to leave profile details out of a new score card and review content before sharing.

Websites and external services

These information pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub and its delivery providers receive normal connection information, including IP addresses and browser headers, to deliver the site. See the GitHub privacy statement. These pages store your appearance preference locally and do not include advertising SDKs, analytics scripts, contact forms or embedded social trackers.

When you open a browser game preview, its hosting service receives connection information needed to serve the game. Local game saves are separate from that website-delivery information.

Links to Apple, Google, X and other services open their websites. Their policies apply to information they receive.

Support messages and retention

If you email us, we receive your email address and anything you include, such as your name, device details, screenshots or an order reference. We use that information to answer your request, investigate problems and handle related privacy or purchase questions. Our email provider also processes correspondence to deliver and store it.

We retain correspondence for as long as needed to handle the request and related service, legal or dispute obligations. You can request deletion by email. Please send only information needed to resolve the issue.

Your choices, deletion and recovery

  • Advertising: when offered, open the coin counter → Your rewards → Ads → Privacy options to revisit Google’s consent options. Availability and choices depend on your region. Device privacy controls and provider privacy tools are also available.
  • Profile: edit and save your name, or select a built-in character and save to replace a custom photo. Play as guest hides the saved profile; it does not erase it.
  • Local records: deleting the app or clearing this game’s browser/site data can remove progress, profiles, coins and coin-unlocked destinations. There is no publisher-operated backup. Device-backup behavior depends on your settings. Read the recovery limits first.
  • Information sent to us: email us to ask about access, correction or deletion of support information, or to raise a privacy concern. Depending on your location, additional privacy rights may apply. We may need enough information to verify and locate your request.

Removing local data does not delete independent records held by Apple, Google, GitHub or your email provider; use their privacy controls for those records. Mandatory legal retention can limit a deletion request.

Families and policy updates

No personal contact details are required to play. Parents and guardians can supervise optional sharing and use device purchase controls. If a child has sent personal information to support, contact us to discuss removal.

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. The date on this page identifies this version. Questions: FangVentures.HQ@gmail.com.
