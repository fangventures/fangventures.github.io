Sort, hold, load, launch

Play in portrait. Load cargo that matches the truck’s color. Use Hold to make room for cargo whose truck has not arrived yet, then send it when it matches. Finish the puzzle to launch the rocket.

The timer counts active play; it is not a countdown. Tap the game name to open settings. Restart asks for confirmation. Optional paid rescues spend the displayed coins only after confirmation; restarting does not refund a rescue.

Coins, purchases and Restore

Earn coins through eligible completed levels and daily claims. When offered in the iOS app, optional coin packs use Apple checkout; the current local price appears before confirmation. Coins unlock destinations or pay for optional rescues. Coins have no cash value, cannot be transferred and are not a subscription.

Remove Ads is a separate, one-time purchase. It removes banners and automatic level-break ads. Optional rewarded videos can remain available. Use Restore purchases in the rewards menu with the same Apple Account to recover an eligible Remove Ads entitlement.

Restore does not rebuild a delivered coin balance, coins already spent, coin-unlocked destinations or local progress. Those records stay on this device. See saves and recovery before deleting the app.

If Apple charged you but coins are missing, reopen the app with a connection and allow pending delivery to finish. Contact us with the Apple order reference if the problem continues; avoid buying again to troubleshoot. Apple handles billing and refund requests, subject to eligibility and your consumer rights.

Why is a purchase or video unavailable?

Availability depends on the build, configured products, connectivity, purchase restrictions, consent and ad inventory. A pre-release build may not offer every purchase or video. The browser preview cannot make Apple purchases or show native AdMob videos. Core sorting remains playable without a purchase or video.

A rewarded video earns coins only when the ad provider confirms the earned reward. Closing early, a failed load or an unavailable video does not award coins.

Saves, offline play and recovery

The installed app bundles its core game resources for offline play. Ads, purchases, support and external sharing services need a connection. Browser offline play depends on resources already cached on that browser.

Progress, personal bests, coins, unlocked destinations, settings and optional profiles are local. There is no game-account login, cloud-save service or automatic synchronization between devices or between the app and browser.

Deleting the app or clearing website data can erase these records. Device backups are controlled by your operating system and are not a game-operated recovery service. Try reopening the app and contact support before reinstalling to troubleshoot. Apple Restore can recover eligible non-consumable ownership, not your local coin wallet.

Ads, profiles and privacy choices

The native app can show a bottom banner and paced ads at completed-level breaks. Google handles required advertising-consent messages. When available, open the coin counter, choose Ads in Your rewards, then Privacy options.

A profile is optional. Names and chosen photos stay on your device until you choose to share them. To remove a custom photo, select a built-in character and save. Play as guest hides the profile but retains the last saved profile. Read the privacy choices and deletion guide for details.

Sharing a score

Choose Share after a mission to prepare a score picture on your device. It can contain your enabled name, avatar and badge as well as the mission result. Use Guest mode if you do not want profile details included. Review the picture before sending it.

The game does not post automatically. Available share destinations depend on your device and installed apps. The X option opens a text/link composer; save the score image and attach it yourself when needed.

Sound, motion and a paused screen

Tap the game name to open settings for sound, music, vibration and Less motion. Check device media volume if audio is quiet. Active-play timing pauses while you are away, in menus or watching a full-screen ad. Turn the device upright if a landscape pause is shown.
